◤ SECTION // 01 · MISSION BRIEFING

A fun space where
fans meet fans.

Star Wars Club at UW is for anyone who loves the galaxy far, far away — and for anyone who's curious and wants a way in. We watch the movies, play the games, swing the sabers, and argue about the lore. No trivia test at the door.

If you've seen everything twice, great. If you've never made it past the opening crawl, also great — a good chunk of the club started exactly there.

◤ SECTION // 02 · COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you show up

Do I need to know Star Wars?

Nope. Plenty of members joined having seen one film or none. Newcomers are the whole point.

How often do you meet?

Usually every other week. Times and rooms get announced in the Discord as they're set.

What happens at a meeting?

It varies — a movie, a game, a lightsaber session, a debate. Members help pick what's next.

Are there dues?

None. Show up, hang out, that's membership.

Can I join mid-quarter?

Any time. There's no cutoff and no application — the Discord link is always open.

Still have a question?

Email us or ask in the Discord — an officer will get back to you.
